## Decisions made
- **Workflow.** I followed TT-019 rule 8. A scratchpad helper paints on a 320×72 pixel canvas: polygons, ellipses, rings, glyph-pattern fills, torch light ramps and an overlay "glaze". It then picks the best glyph and ink/paper pair for each cell. Detail is drawn as cell overrides: lettering, gate bars `┃`, tally marks `╫`, tiles `┼`, numbers, `O─O` for the handcuffs, and `S` for the curly cord.
  - The converter keeps black sky (air → `' '`) apart from black solids (`█` ink 0). Lightning therefore outlines silhouettes. I checked this with a map of which cells the lightning lights.
- **`harrows_car`, right-hand drive.** The steering wheel is on the right and the handcuffs are on the passenger seat on the left. The brief says "passenger seat right", but Harrow's car is a UK Cortina.
- **Keeping rain inside the windscreen.** `fx.js` rains on every `' '` and `█` cell. In `harrows_car`, the solid cells below the windscreen are therefore drawn as `▓` with ink = paper. They look identical, and the rain fx skips them, so it never falls on the dashboard or seats.
  - If the reviewer prefers a proper fx mask in `fx.js`, that is a follow-up.
- **Lettering** (all capitals, as the L11 lint and tests require): `ASHWORTH` on the mill gates, `NO CREDIT WITHOUT THE BOOK` in the counting house, `PLEASE RING` over the reception hatch, `P-R` on the cabinet, `1968` on the quarry hut calendar, and drawer numbers 1–8 in the morgue.
- **Spectrum check.** Every key edge has a contrast that is not `b/6` or `c/f`. For example, the tally stone is `b` with `f` marks, giving blue against white.
